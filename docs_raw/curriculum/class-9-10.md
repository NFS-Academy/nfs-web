# Class 9-10 Plan

## Subjects

- Physics
- Chemistry
- Mathematics
- Higher Mathematics
- Biology

## Initial Simulation Priorities

1. Chemistry MVP: dry cell/electrochemical behavior, acid-base neutralization,
   precipitation reaction.
2. Physics Chapter 3: force, inertia, momentum, collision, Newton's laws,
   gravity, friction.

## Content Standard

Each chapter should be broken into concepts. Each concept can have one or more
simulations, formulas, explanations, and practice questions.

## Board Relevance

Planning should focus on NCTB/SSC style formulas, scenarios, unit conversions,
problem statements, and common mistakes.

## Physics Initial Chapter

Use Physics Chapter 3 as the first detailed Physics pack.

Concepts:

- Inertia and Newton's first law.
- Force and force classification.
- Fundamental forces.
- Momentum.
- Collision and conservation of momentum.
- Newton's second law.
- Gravitational force, `g`, and weight.
- Newton's third law.
- Frictional force.

## Chemistry Initial Chapter Set

Use three starter simulations:

- Dry cell / electrochemical behavior.
- Acid-base neutralization.
- Precipitation reaction.

Keep dry-cell behavior separate from salt-bridge galvanic cell behavior unless a
comparison simulation explicitly explains the difference.

## Placeholder Subjects

Mathematics, Higher Mathematics, and Biology should be present in the catalog
as planned subjects, but should not claim completed simulations until source
chapters are added.
