# Slide Calipers

Understanding the vernier scale and how to measure the diameter of a solid sphere using Slide Calipers.

## The Formula

Length $L$ is calculated as:

$$ L = M + V \times VC $$

Where:
- $M$ = Main scale reading
- $V$ = Vernier scale coincidence
- $VC$ = Vernier constant
