# Naim Science Lab - Comprehensive Curriculum Map
## Subject: Physics (Class 9-10 / O-Levels)

This document maps out the exhaustive conceptual framework for the entire Physics curriculum. It dictates exactly where interactive simulations (current and future) integrate into the NetAcad-style learning platform.

---

### Chapter 1: Physical Quantities and Measurement
**Core Concept:** Understanding the foundation of physics, fundamental and derived quantities, SI units, and the mechanics of measuring instruments (Slide Calipers, Screw Gauge).
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `1.1-Slide-Calipers`: Interactive measurement lab.
  - `1.2-Screw-Gauge`: Micrometer measurement lab.

### Chapter 2: Motion
**Core Concept:** Kinematics, scalar vs. vector quantities, distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration, and equations of motion (s=vt, v=u+at, etc.). Falling bodies and Galileo's laws.
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `2.1-Equations-of-Motion`: Graphing v-t and s-t curves.
  - `2.2-Free-Fall-Gravity`: Dropping objects in a vacuum vs air.

### Chapter 3: Force
**Core Concept:** Dynamics, Newton's three laws of motion, inertia, momentum, conservation of momentum, and friction (static vs. kinetic).
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `3.1-Newtons-Laws-Lab`: Interactive force push/pull.
  - `3.2-Conservation-of-Momentum`: Collision simulator.

### Chapter 4: Work, Power and Energy
**Core Concept:** Work done by a constant force, kinetic and potential energy, conservation of energy, power, and efficiency of systems.
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `4.1-Energy-Skate-Park`: Kinetic to potential conversion.
  - `4.2-Pulleys-and-Efficiency`: Work and power calculation.

---

### Chapter 5: State of Matter and Pressure
**Core Concept:** Understanding how solids, liquids, and gases exert pressure. The mathematical principles governing fluids, density, depth, and buoyant force.
- **Simulations (Active/Discovered):**
  - `5.1-Buoyancy-Lab`: Density & Buoyant force. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/5.1-Buoyancy-Lab (Private)]**
  - `5.2-Pascals-Law`: Hydraulic presses and pressure transmission. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/5.2-Pascals-Law (Private)]**
  - `5.3-Archimedes-principle`: Displaced fluid and weight loss. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/5.3-Archimedes-principle (Public)]**
  - `5.4-Torricelli-Experiment`: Atmospheric pressure and barometers. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/5.4-Torricelli-Experiment (Private)]**

### Chapter 6: Effect of Heat on Matter
**Core Concept:** Thermodynamics, how thermal energy transfers, macroscopic expansion of matter, specific heat capacity, and the conservation of thermal energy.
- **Simulations (Active/Discovered):**
  - `6.1-Thermal-Expansion`: Linear, superficial, and cubical expansion of solids. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/6.1-Thermal-Expansion (Public)]**
  - `6.2-Liquid-Real-Apparent-Expansion`: Anomalous expansion and real vs apparent volume change. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/6.2-Liquid-Real-Apparent-Expansion (Public)]**
  - `6.3-Specific-Heat`: Heat capacity, specific heat, and temperature change. -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/6.3-Specific-Heat (Public)]**
  - `6.4-Principle-of-Heat-Exchange`: Calorimetry and thermal equilibrium (Heat lost = Heat gained). -> **[REPO: NFS-Academy/6.4-Principle-of-Heat-Exchange (Private)]**

---

### Chapter 7: Waves and Sound
**Core Concept:** Simple harmonic motion, transverse vs longitudinal waves, characteristics of sound, echo, and velocity of sound in different mediums.
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `7.1-Wave-Interference`: Transverse wave generator.
  - `7.2-Echo-and-Sonar`: Reflection of sound waves.

### Chapter 8: Reflection of Light
**Core Concept:** Nature of light, laws of reflection, plane mirrors, spherical mirrors (concave/convex), and image formation (real vs virtual).
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `8.1-Ray-Optics-Reflection`: Interactive ray tracing for mirrors.

### Chapter 9: Refraction of Light
**Core Concept:** Laws of refraction, refractive index, total internal reflection, optical fibers, and lenses (convex/concave).
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `9.1-Snells-Law`: Refraction across boundaries.
  - `9.2-Lens-Image-Formation`: Focal lengths and magnification.

### Chapter 10: Static Electricity
**Core Concept:** Electric charge, Coulomb's Law, electric field, electric potential, and capacitors.
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `10.1-Coulombs-Law`: Point charge interactions.
  - `10.2-Electric-Field-Lines`: Visualizing fields.

### Chapter 11: Current Electricity
**Core Concept:** Electric current, electromotive force (EMF), Ohm's law, resistance, series/parallel circuits, and electrical power.
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `11.1-Circuit-Builder`: Interactive schematic drawing and testing.
  - `11.2-Ohms-Law`: Voltage, current, resistance graphing.

### Chapter 12: Magnetic Effect of Current
**Core Concept:** Magnets, magnetic fields created by current, electromagnetic induction, motors, transformers, and generators.
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `12.1-Electromagnetic-Induction`: Faraday's Law.
  - `12.2-Transformer-Lab`: Step-up vs Step-down.

### Chapter 13: Modern Physics and Electronics
**Core Concept:** Radioactivity, alpha/beta/gamma decay, half-life, semiconductors, diodes, and logic gates (AND, OR, NOT).
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `13.1-Radioactive-Decay`: Half-life simulator.
  - `13.2-Logic-Gates`: Digital circuit builder.

### Chapter 14: Physics to Save Life
**Core Concept:** Medical physics and diagnostic instruments (X-ray, Ultrasonography, CT Scan, MRI, ECG, Endoscopy).
- **Simulations (Planned):**
  - `14.1-Medical-Imaging`: Interactive diagnostic matching.
